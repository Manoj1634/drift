# Water Cooler — Judging Strategy

## Rubric

### 1. It Works — 40 points

To maximize:
- use one real market
- use real source data where practical
- keep fixtures as fail-safe
- make Grok return structured output
- rehearse the exact demo path at least five times
- stop adding features before the final reliability window

Judge takeaway:
> This is a functioning end-to-end product, not a slide deck.

---

### 2. Taste — 30 points

To maximize:
- make the divergence visually obvious
- minimize dashboard clutter
- use one hero signal
- make `Why?` the central interaction
- make the historical replay the memorable moment
- show sources elegantly
- animate agent progress only if reliable

Judge takeaway:
> I immediately understood what changed and why it mattered.

---

### 3. Business Use Case — 30 points

To maximize:
- state the manual workflow clearly
- explain the user as a prediction-market participant
- show how the product reduces research fragmentation
- show that the recommendation is inspectable
- demonstrate expansion after the working vertical, not before it

Judge takeaway:
> I understand who would use this and why.

---

## Grok Requirement

Grok must be visibly load-bearing.

Use Grok for:
- X/web evidence discovery
- cross-source reasoning
- conflict detection
- explanation
- counterargument

Avoid:
- hardcoded divergence logic with Grok only rewriting text

---

## First 60 Seconds

The demo must reach this moment quickly:

```text
Market Probability: 62%

External signals: rising

Water Cooler:
HIGH DIVERGENCE
78 / 100
```

Then:

`View Why`

---

## Best Wow Feature

**Historical Replay**

Why it scores well:
- proves the system works on a complete timeline
- gives the demo strong visual storytelling
- makes the business value concrete
- shows the reasoning before the outcome reveal
- avoids depending on a live Netflix update during judging

---

## Claims Discipline

Say:
- divergence signal
- evidence gap
- external evidence
- potential mismatch
- suggested side

Do not say:
- guaranteed winner
- guaranteed profit
- manipulation detected
- market is wrong

---

## Priority Order

1. End-to-end reliability
2. Clear divergence moment
3. Grok reasoning
4. Source transparency
5. Historical replay
6. Visual polish
7. Everything else

Do not reverse this order.
